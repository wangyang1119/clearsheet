ClearSheet

Turn marked-up worksheets and exam papers into clean, print-ready practice sheets in seconds.

100% On-Device · No Subscriptions

Key Features

  • Smart Ink & Highlighter Removal: Automatically filters red, blue, and green pen marks as well as yellow highlighter while preserving printed questions.
  • Print-Ready Scan Enhancement: Removes shadows and paper tints for crisp black text on a clean white background.
  • Precision Manual Touch-Up: Fine-tune remaining marks with single-finger Box Select, Eraser Brush, two-finger zoom, and Hold-to-Compare.
  • HD Image & A4 PDF Export: Save high-resolution images or export clean A4 practice PDFs ready for printing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my worksheets uploaded to any server?
No. All image processing runs 100% locally on your iPhone or iPad. Your documents never leave your device.
How does pricing work?
You can clean up to 5 worksheets for free. ClearSheet Lifetime Pro is a single one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited cleaning and A4 PDF exports forever—no recurring subscriptions.
How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Open the upgrade screen in ClearSheet and tap "Restore Purchases" while signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.
