Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

ClearSheet is designed with privacy first. All document scanning, handwriting removal, and PDF generation happen 100% locally on your device.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage analytics, or document images. ClearSheet does not require an account or login.

2. Camera & Photo Library Access

  • Camera: Used solely to scan worksheets and exam papers directly on your device.
  • Photo Library: Used solely to import existing worksheet photos you select and to save cleaned HD images back to your library when you choose to export.

No images or documents are ever uploaded to external servers or third-party services.

3. In-App Purchases

ClearSheet offers a one-time Lifetime Pro upgrade processed securely through Apple's App Store. We do not have access to your payment details or Apple ID billing information.

4. Third-Party Services

ClearSheet does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, tracking libraries, or analytics tools.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at startfrom2026@163.com.
